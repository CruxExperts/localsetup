# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want LocalSetup to be useful to people who care about safe, reviewable agent workflows. Contributors and maintainers are expected to keep discussion respectful, practical, and focused on the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing experience levels, tools, platforms, and constraints.
- Keep technical criticism specific and actionable.
- Assume good faith, while still taking safety, security, and privacy seriously.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sharing secrets, exploit details, hostnames, or private logs in public issues.
- Repeatedly ignoring maintainer guidance about scope, safety, or disclosure.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close or lock issues; or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors. Security-sensitive concerns should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public issue discussion.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is intentionally short and project-specific. It is inspired by the Contributor Covenant's practical expectations for open source communities.
